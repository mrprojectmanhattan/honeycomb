Honeycomb is a gentle daily check-in app for the basics: mood, water, sleep,
movement, and the habits and goals you are working on. It is built for someone
who forgets things and wants the app to do the remembering, without nagging.

Everything you log is a row in a local database on your phone and nowhere else.
There is no account, no cloud, no analytics, and no ads. The app has no INTERNET
permission at all, so it cannot phone home even by accident.

Features

* Mood log: a quick 1 to 5 check-in with an optional note and tags. An optional
  hourly reminder nudges you; you can also log any time.
* Journal: short written or voice reflections, grouped by day. After logging a
  mood the app can offer to open a reflection for that day.
* Water: a honey-jar tracker with a daily goal and quick-add servings.
* Sleep: log last night by bedtime and wake time, with an optional assist that
  reads only charger connect and disconnect events (off by default).
* Workout: a weekly muscle-group split shown as a figure made of hexagons.
* Goals and habits: day-to-day goals (some tracked automatically from your other
  logs), long-horizon "major goals" with a home-screen reminder, and quit or
  started streak counters.
* Trends and history: a honeycomb calendar of the last 30 days, per-day and
  per-hour averages, tag breakdowns, sleep and workout summaries, and a full
  browsable history with edit and delete.
* Step counter: optional, off by default, a one-shot read of the motion sensor
  with nothing running in the background.
* CSV export of everything, any time.

Privacy

The two optional helpers (sleep guess from charger events, step count from the
motion sensor) are off until you turn them on, read only what is described, and
are forgotten the moment you turn them off. The app never reads app usage,
location, contacts, the microphone (except when you record a voice reflection),
or anything about what you do.
